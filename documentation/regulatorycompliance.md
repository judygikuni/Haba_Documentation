# Regulatory and Compliance Requirements


## Overview

- Compliance with laws and regulations is crucial to ensure the safety, privacy, and trust of our users and vendors on the Haba platform. This section explains the legal frameworks we follow and how they shape the way the product works.

- Compliance means we meet the rules set by governments and regulatory bodies, protecting user rights, preventing fraud, and enabling us to operate legally and ethically in Kenya’s fast-growing e-commerce and fintech market

## Key Regulations that Guide Us

| Regulations | Applies To | What it means for Haba |
| : --- :| : --- : | : --- : |
| **Kenya Data Protection Act (DPA), 2019** | Handling of user data, including AI-generated data, payments | User consent, data security, limited data collection, transparency |
| **Central Bank of Kenya (CBK) Fintech Guidelines** | Digital payments, vendor payout, and group buying | Fraud preventation, vendor verification, payment security|
| **Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act (Kenya)** | Data security and AI safety | Secure AI chatbot, protected database, and fraud preventation |
| General Data Protection | Global best practices | Data privacy standards |


## How We Address Compliance in Haba

- We collect only the minimum necessary data
- We keep transaction records securely 
- Only verified vendors are allowed in our application to reduce fraud risk
- We facilitate payment through secure channels (M-Pesa)
- Necessary licenses and permits are obtained as required.
- We maintain adequate risk management systems consistent with financial and operational regulations.
