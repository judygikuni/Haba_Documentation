# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

## For Mama Mboga Vendors

### 1. How do I register as a Mama Mboga on the Gorzo platform?
- Download the mobile app from the phone store
- Click on the **Register** button and fill in your details including shop name, location, and contact information.
- Verify your account as instructed to complete registration.

### 2. How do I add or update my product inventory?
- Log in to your vendor app.
- Go to the **Inventory** section.
- To add a new product, click **Add Product**, fill in the details, and save.
- To update existing products, select the product, modify the details, and save changes.

### 3. What if I run out of stock on a product?
- Update your inventory promptly to reflect zero quantity or remove the product temporarily.
- This ensures customers only see available products and reduces cancellations.

### 4. How do I know when I have new orders?
- You will receive notifications on the PWA dashboard.
- Check the **Orders** section regularly to track new group and individual orders.

### 5. How does payment work?
- Payments are processed via M-Pesa using STK Push, which sends payment requests directly to customers.
- Check the **Payments** section in your dashboard to confirm received payments before preparing orders.

### 6. Can I communicate with customers through Gorzo?
- Currently, there is no in-app messaging system. Communication is handled outside the app.

### 7. What if I face technical issues or need help?

## For Customers

### 1. How do I sign up for the Gorzo app?
- Download the Gorzo app from your device’s app store.
- Tap **Sign Up** and enter the required personal information.
- Verify your account and log in.

### 2. How do I join a group order?
- Go to the **Groups** section.
- Browse live group orders near you.
- Select a group and add your order before the joining deadline.
- Confirm your participation.

### 3. What happens if the group order minimum is not met?
- The group order will be dissolved, and no orders will be placed.
- You can join or create another group.

### 4. How do I pay for my orders?
- Payment requests are sent via M-Pesa STK Push to your mobile phone.
- Complete the payment promptly to secure your order.

### 5. Can I cancel or change my order after placing it?
- Orders cannot be changed or cancelled once the group order closes.
- For individual orders, please contact the vendor directly outside the app.

### 6. How do I pick up my groceries?
- After payment confirmation, pick up your items at the vendor’s designated location.
- Details will be provided in your order confirmation or notification.

### 7. What should I do if I have a problem with my order?
- Contact Gorzo support at [Insert Support Contact Info].
- You can also check the vendor’s contact details in the app for direct communication.

### 8. Is my personal data safe when using Gorzo?
- Yes. We adhere to regulatory and compliance standards to protect your data.
- For details, visit the [Regulatory and Compliance](regulatory-compliance.md) page.

For detailed guides, please proceed to the [Getting Started](getting-started.md) section.
